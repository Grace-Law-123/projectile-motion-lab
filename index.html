Analysis
  • In frictionless theory, 45° yields maximum range. With air drag, the optimal angle is typically less than 45° because horizontal velocity continuously decays.
  • Higher angles produce greater max height and longer flight time, but not necessarily greater range.
  • Landing speed with drag is lower than initial speed (energy dissipation). The difference grows with steeper angles.
